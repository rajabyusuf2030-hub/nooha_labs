# PRD: Nooha Official Website

## Vision
The public face of Nooha Labs, showcasing our ecosystem of products and services.

## Goals
- Establish brand authority.
- Generate leads for Nooha ERP and AI services.
- Provide a platform for company news and blogs.

## Target Users
- School administrators.
- Business owners seeking automation.
- Technology partners.

## Features
- Dynamic product showcases.
- Blog system.
- Contact and quote request forms.
- Careers portal.

## Success Metrics
- Page load speed (Lighthouse score 95+).
- Lead conversion rate.
- SEO ranking for target keywords.
